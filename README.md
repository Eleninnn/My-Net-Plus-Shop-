# My-SMP-Plus-Shop-
:fire: Guide For The Plus Shop :fire:

The Plus Shop is a shop that takes Playerpoints :coin: as a currency to buy netherite tools and armors, 

However there are certain tools :pick: named plus that has a time limit of 24 hours after first use, it will be self-terminated after the 24 hour limit,

And the tools that is named just Netherite are permanents, such as Netherite Armors, Netherite Tools,

This Plus Shop benefits the use of Playerpoints rather than spending it on spawners, it provides more variety of stuff you can buy with Playerpoints 

Here are the lists of ways you can get Playerpoints:
1. For every 15 minutes, there is a trivia challenges that requires a player to answer, (**but rewards are only given to 1st, 2nd, 3rd, player that answers it, below that won't get anything**)

2. killing players in duels or outside duels grants you 10 Playerpoints, but a player has a cooldown (**so players won't farm kills with an alt**)

3. Players are given 1 Playerpoints for every 10 minutes,


Warning :warning: buying plus tools from Auction House :house: needs to have it's own precautions, as players needs to check if it has been used first or not, because if it has been used it will be self-terminated in under 24 hours, because it was already actived beforehand,

The prices of all the tools and armors in The Plus Shop:

Plus Pickaxe: (**Silk Touch and Fortune Same Price**) 1,500 Playerpoints (24 hour)

Plus Axe: (**Sell Axe and Tree Axe Same Price**) 1,500 Playerpoints (24 hour)

Plus Shovel: (**Normal And Silk Touch Same Price**) 1,500 Playerpoints (24 hour)

Mace: (**Wind Burst 3**) 1000 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Netherite Armor: (**Seperate into 4 Not Full Set**) Each Piece 750 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Netherite Hoe: (**Silk Touch**) 250 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Netherite Sword: (**Sharpness V**) 750 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Netherite Spear: (**Sharpness V**) 750 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Netherite Shovel: (**Efficiency V**) 400 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Netherite Axe: (**Efficiency V**) 300 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Netherite Pickaxe: (**Fortune And Silk Touch Same Price**) 500 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Crossbow: (**Piercing IV**) 250 Playerpoints (Permanent) 

Bow (**Power V**) 250 Playerpoints (Permanent)

Players might be thinking, what are the differences between "Plus Tools and Netherite Tools?" the answer to that is the Plus Tools have an advantage beyond Vanilla, such as 9 blocks broken with a single break with the Plus Shovel, and selling stuff in a chest with the Plus Sell Axe, 

And the Netherite Tools are Vannila Tools that are maxed out to the Vannila limit,

Keeps the server balanced with the 24 hour limit with the Plus Tools.
